Jellyfin Review

Published on November 5, 2026

Jellyfin is a free media server platform that enables users to save, organize, and play their personal media collections on computers, TV, phone, and other supported devices.

It is much like a media center platform, such as Plex or Emby, for functionality, yet it places more emphasis on privacy and user control. If I were to describe Jellyfin simply, I'd say it's a personal home media server that allows you to have complete control over your movies, TV shows, music, and other media files.

Instead of depending solely on streaming subscriptions, it empowers you to arrange and stream your own collection across various devices. One of the main reasons users get attracted to it during a Jellyfin review search is that, in reality, it is completely free and open-source. You don't have to be forced to subscribe just to access your own content, and for many media users, this is a great plus.

Podman Review

Published on November 5, 2026

Podman stands for "Pod Manager" and is a Red Hat-designed open-source container and containerized application management tool. In general, it is a good alternative to Docker, especially for those with security concerns or looking for greater flexibility in use. It works on the concept of a Daemonless mode, meaning there is no central daemon for the containers. This daemonless architecture not only enhances security by running containers as rootless (non-root) users but also does away with the overhead of maintaining containers through a container engine based on a daemon.
